# Decision memo

For any decision the founder must make (an approval gate, a direction change, a trade-off between goals):

1. **Question** in one sentence, ending with a question mark, plus the deadline.
2. **Recommendation** first, in one sentence.
3. **Options** (2-4, including "do nothing"): what happens, cost, time, risk, reversibility.
4. **Evidence**: facts with sources; label opinions as opinions.
5. **Risks of the recommendation**, and how we would notice early.
6. **What would change my mind**: the specific fact or result that flips the recommendation.

One page. Save to `company/memos/<date>-<slug>.md` and link it from the approval request.
