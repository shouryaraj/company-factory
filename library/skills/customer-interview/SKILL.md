# Customer interviews

## Prepare (`customers/interviews/script-<topic>.md`)
- 3-5 hypotheses to test, each falsifiable.
- Questions about the past and present ("Tell me about the last time...", "What did you do then?", "What did that cost?"), never "Would you use...?".
- Ask what they already built or pay for, and who decides on budget.
- End with: "Who else should I talk to?"

## Synthesize (only from notes the founder provides)
1. One file per interview: `customers/interviews/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<company>.md`, keeping verbatim quotes in quotation marks.
2. Update `customers/insights.md`: per hypothesis, supported / contradicted / untested with counts ("4 of 7").
3. Highlight surprises and contradicting evidence first.
4. Recommend the next questions and who to talk to next.
