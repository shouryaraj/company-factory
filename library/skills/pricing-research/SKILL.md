# Pricing research

1. **Value metric.** What unit grows with the value customers get (seats, usage, projects, revenue)? List 2-3 candidates and why.
2. **Market.** Table of 5-10 alternatives, including "do nothing" and spreadsheets: price points, value metric, free tier, tiers, source URL and the date you saw it.
3. **Evidence.** Collect willingness-to-pay signals from `customers/` (interviews, quotes): only real ones, cited. Note the gaps.
4. **Options.** 2-3 pricing models, each with tiers, prices, what is gated, who it is for, rough revenue at 10/100/1,000 customers, and risks.
5. **Recommend one**, with the cheapest experiment to test it (pricing-page test, quote it in five sales calls, pilot price).

Save to `research/pricing-<date>.md`. Changing live prices is a Money gate.
