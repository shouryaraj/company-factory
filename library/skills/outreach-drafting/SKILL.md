# Outreach drafting

For each prospect:
1. Find one real, specific, sourced reason to write (a launch, a post, a job ad, a product detail). Cite it.
2. Write at most 90 words: the specific reason, the problem you think they may have (as a question), one sentence on who the founder is, one small ask (a 20-minute call to learn how they handle it; not a pitch).
3. No flattery, no fake familiarity, no invented mutual connections, no claims the product cannot back.
4. Give a subject line and one follow-up (sent after 5 business days).

Batch all drafts in `customers/outreach/<YYYY-MM-DD>-batch.md` with a table (company, contact role, channel, reason, source) and add one approval request covering the batch. Never send.
