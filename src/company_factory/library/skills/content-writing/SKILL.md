# Content writing

At the top of every draft: channel, audience, goal, call to action.

1. Lead with the reader's problem in their words, then what the product does about it, then proof (a demo, a command, a screenshot path).
2. Show, don't claim: real commands and real output beat adjectives.
3. Match the channel: forum posts are plain and helpful; launch posts are short with one link; docs are task-first.
4. Check every claim against the product and the decisions log. Remove anything not true today.
5. Save to `marketing/drafts/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<channel>-<slug>.md` and request approval to publish.
