# Competitor / market research

1. Restate the question and what decision it feeds.
2. Search broadly first (several queries in parallel), then go to primary sources: company sites, docs, pricing pages, funding announcements, GitHub.
3. For each company capture: what they sell, to whom, pricing model, funding/stage, open source or not, strengths, gaps relevant to us, source URL, date seen.
4. Grade each claim: `verified` (primary source), `secondary` (press/directory), `unverified`.
5. Output to `research/<topic>.md`: answer first, then a table, then implications, then open questions.

Directories and listicles are leads, not facts. Never contact the companies.
